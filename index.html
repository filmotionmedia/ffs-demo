Core Engineering Capabilities

WHAT WE
DELIVER.

From PE-stamped calculations to same-day Bay Area hardware delivery, FSS operates as a fully integrated engineering and supply partner on your most demanding projects.

CAP — 01

Seismic Calculations & PE Stamping

Full IBC and CBC-compliant seismic bracing calculations, signed and stamped by a licensed California Professional Engineer. AHJ-ready submittals on every project.

CAP — 02

Equipment Anchorage Design

Precise anchorage engineering for all MEP equipment — AHUs, chillers, pumps, electrical switchgear, and more. Sized for seismic zone requirements and slab conditions.

CAP — 03

Vibration Isolation & Noise Dampening

Engineering solutions for rotating equipment vibration — spring isolators, inertia bases, and flexible connectors — protecting structural integrity and occupant comfort.

CAP — 04

BIM / Revit 3D Modeling

Full 3D coordination in Revit and AutoCAD — clash detection, coordinated MEP routing, and construction-ready documentation that bridges engineering and the field seamlessly.
